New to fund analysis? How to analyse a mutual fund in 8 steps
  1. Start with your goal and time horizon, not the fund.Money needed within 1 year belongs in Liquid, Money Market or Arbitrage funds. 1–3 years: short-duration debt or Conservative Hybrid. 5 years and more: equity. The horizon decides the category; only then pick funds inside it.Use: the asset-class tabs and sub-category chips
  2. Compare a fund only with its own category.A small-cap fund returning 18% is not "better" than a large-cap fund returning 14%; it takes more risk. Every ranking here is within the category, against the category median and the category's benchmark.Use: the Overview tab, then a category; check the "Category median" and benchmark rows
  3. Look at consistency before headline returns.A great last year can hide a weak record. Rolling 3-year returns show how often the fund beat its benchmark across hundreds of start dates, and how bad its worst 3-year stretch was.Use: Consistency view, "Rolling 3Y % beat BM" and "Rolling 3Y worst"
  4. Check returns per unit of risk.Sharpe and Sortino show return earned for the volatility taken; higher is better. Standard deviation and max drawdown show how bumpy the ride was and how far it fell from a peak.Use: Risk (3Y) view: Sharpe, Sortino, Std dev, Max drawdown
  5. See how it behaves in up and down markets.Upside capture above 100% means it rises more than the index; downside capture below 100% means it falls less. The best funds combine both. Alpha is the extra return after adjusting for market risk.Use: Risk (3Y) view: Upside capture, Downside capture, Alpha
  6. Read the calendar years for bad-year behaviour.Look at years like 2018, 2020 or 2022. Did the fund protect capital better than its index, or fall harder? Year-by-year results also reveal one lucky year propping up the average.Use: Calendar years view, or the bar chart in any fund's profile
  7. Check costs, size and the manager.Lower TER compounds in your favour every year; always use the Direct plan. Very large AUM can make small and mid-cap funds harder to manage. A recent fund-manager change means past returns may say less about the future.Use: Cost & size view: TER Direct, AUM, Lead manager tenure
  8. Decide, then ask follow-up questions.Shortlist 2–4 funds, tick "Compare" to see them side by side, and use Ask AI in a fund's profile for a plain-English review of its strengths, weaknesses and who it suits. Treat it as analysis, not personal advice.Use: Compare tray at the bottom, and Ask AI in each fund profile
How the numbers are calculated
  • Universe: every open-ended Direct Growth scheme in AMFI's daily NAV file with a NAV in October 2026. NAV history from mfapi.in; equity benchmarks are Total Return Indices from niftyindices.com.
  • Trailing returns: point to point on the last NAV on or before each date. Under 1 year is absolute; 1 year and above is CAGR. Calendar years run 31 Dec to 31 Dec; the current year is year to date.
  • Risk ratios use 36 (or 60) month-end returns to the last month end, annualised, with a 6.0% risk-free rate for every fund. Capture ratios use the geometric average of fund and benchmark returns in up and down months (Morningstar method).
  • Benchmarks: the AMFI tier-1 index for each equity category, a matching sector index for sectoral and thematic funds, and the tracked index for passive funds where available. Debt, hybrid, FoF and solution funds are compared with the equal-weighted average of their own category, because their CRISIL indices are not freely published.
  • Rolling 3Y: CAGR for every daily start date; shown only with at least one year of start dates. Shading marks peer quartile within the funds on screen: darker is better. For risk, lower volatility, lower downside capture and smaller drawdowns count as better; beta is not shaded.
  • TER comes from AMFI's daily TER disclosure: "TER Direct" is the base expense ratio (the figure factsheets quote); "incl. costs" adds brokerage, transaction costs and statutory levies. AUM is AMFI's quarterly average AUM across all plans. Fund managers, managing-since dates, the official benchmark, riskometer and exit load come from each scheme's SEBI Scheme Summary Document, refreshed weekly.
  • Documents: factsheet and monthly-portfolio links are each AMC's official pages as registered with AMFI, checked monthly by an automated link test. Not yet in the tables: holdings, and YTM / duration for debt funds. Rows tagged "plan inferred" come from AMFI rows with no plan label, where the higher-NAV growth row is taken as Direct.
